01 / THE GROUP

A place to learn.
A platform to create.

Buddha Group of Institutions (BGI), located at CL-1, Sector 7, GIDA, Gorakhpur, brings together institutions offering professional education across technology, management, pharmacy and other disciplines.

The group’s stated educational approach emphasizes academic learning, practical exposure, co-curricular activities and the development of communication, leadership and problem-solving skills.
